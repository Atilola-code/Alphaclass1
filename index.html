classes of food

six classes of food

carbohydrates

carbohydrates are essential to the body. they are sugars or starch that provides energy for all cells and tissues.

  1. rice
  2. pasta
  3. bread
  4. yam
  5. potatoes

water

water is the most important nutrient for the body. A person can only survive few days without taking water

  1. rain water
  2. tap water
  3. stream water
  4. lake water
  5. river water

protein

Proteins are essential nutrients for the human body. They are one of the building blocks of body tissue and can also serve as a fuel source

  1. meat
  2. fish
  3. eggs
  4. milk
  5. beans

vitamins

Much like vitamins, minerals help support the body. They're essential for many body functions, including building strong bones and teeth

  • orange
  • strawberry
  • carrot
  • apricot
  • bell pepper

fats

They're essential for many body functions, including building strong bones and teeth.

  • avocado
  • cheese
  • dark chocolate
  • oil
  • butter

fiber

Fiber which is essential for digestion, is found in fruits and vegetables.
